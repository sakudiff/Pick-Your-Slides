About

Made by a student who got tired of slide-claiming in the group chat.

Every group presentation starts the same way. Someone posts the deck, someone else pastes a list of names and slide numbers, and everyone edits it by hand until two people end up with slide 4. Pick your slides replaces that with one link.

How it is built to behave

Free, with no sign-in

Open the link, pick your name, claim your slides. No accounts, no ads, nothing to install.

Private by default

PDFs, PowerPoints and member-list screenshots are read in your browser. They are never uploaded.

Gone on its own

A board is deleted after 90 days without changes, and slide previews 30 days after you present.

Open to audit

All the code is public under the MIT license, so anyone can check what it does with your group's data.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There are no accounts, plans or ads.

Do people sign in?

No. Each person opens the link and picks their own name from the list the organizer typed.

Who can edit?

Anyone with the member link can claim, rate and vote, so share it only with your group. Only the organizer link can edit slides, free someone's claim, apply a split or delete the board.

When is a board deleted?

After 90 days without changes. Slide previews go 30 days after the presentation date. The organizer can delete a board at any time.

What happens to my files and screenshots?

PDF, PowerPoint and screenshot reading all happen in your browser. A Google Slides link is fetched as a PDF and passed straight to your browser. Only the titles, names and small slide previews you confirm are saved to the board.

What does Canva access?

Nothing for now. Canva import is off until Canva approves this site, so export the design as a PDF (Share, Download, PDF). Once it is on, Canva asks you to let this site read one design's title and page count and export it as a PDF, and the access token is kept for at most an hour. See the privacy notes.
